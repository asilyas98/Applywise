How it works

  1. Paste your resume here once.
  2. Add a job and paste its description.
  3. Open the job to run a fit score, draft a cover letter, tailor your bullets, and prep interview answers.
  4. Move each job through the pipeline as you hear back.

The AI only uses what's in your resume. It won't invent jobs or skills you don't have, so the more detail you include, the better the output.
