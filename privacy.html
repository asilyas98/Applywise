Offer Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026 · Offer Desk is published by SimpleAI LLC

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Content you add: your name, target roles, resume, work experience notes, the jobs you track (company, role, job description, notes, dates, status) and cover letters you save. Chat messages are used to generate replies and are kept only in the current app session.
  • Usage counts: how many AI requests you make each day, so we can apply fair-use limits.

We don't collect your location or contacts, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the app has no ads.

How it's used

Your account, profile, tracked jobs, and saved cover letters are stored so you can see them on any device you sign in on. When you tap an AI feature (career assistant chat, fit score, cover letter, resume tweaks, interview prep, or filling job details), the app sends your prompt or chat message and the relevant resume, experience notes, and saved job details to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the Claude AI model, to generate the result. Chat messages are not saved to your account by Offer Desk. This processing only happens after you allow AI sharing, and you can turn it off at any time in Account. Anthropic processes the request to return the result and doesn't use API data to train its models under its commercial terms.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase stores your account and content.
  • Netlify runs the server functions that handle AI requests.
  • Anthropic generates AI results, as described above.

We don't sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database access rules keep each person's data visible only to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any job or letter in the app. To delete everything, open Account → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all of your content right away.

Children

Offer Desk is not directed to children under 13.
